FrontBack ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1.2 Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do NOT collect personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
  • We do NOT collect usage analytics or crash reports
  • We do NOT use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs
  • We do NOT transmit any data to external servers
  • We do NOT require account registration or login

2. How We Use Your Information

FrontBack uses device permissions and data solely for the following purposes:

  • Camera: To enable simultaneous dual-camera photo and video capture functionality
  • Microphone: To record audio alongside video content (optional feature)
  • Photo Library: To save your created content for personal use on your device

3. Data Storage & Security

3.1 Local Storage Only

All photos and videos created with FrontBack are stored exclusively on your device. We do not upload, transmit, or store any user-generated content on external servers or cloud services.

3.2 No Server Communication

FrontBack operates entirely offline. The application does not:

  • Send data to our servers
  • Connect to third-party services
  • Require internet connectivity for core features

3.3 Data Encryption

All media files are saved using iOS's standard secure storage mechanisms, protected by your device's encryption and security features.

4. Third-Party Services

FrontBack does not integrate any third-party services.

We do not use:

  • Analytics services
  • Advertising networks
  • Social media integration
  • Third-party cloud storage
  • Crash reporting services

5. Permissions Explained

FrontBack requests the following permissions when you use specific features:

Permission When Requested Can Be Denied?
Camera When launching the camera view No - Required for core functionality
Microphone When recording video with audio Yes - Video will be silent if denied
Photo Library When saving captured media Yes - You can choose not to save

You can manage these permissions at any time in: Settings → Privacy → [Permission]

6. Children's Privacy

FrontBack is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately, and we will take steps to delete such information.

7. Your Rights & Choices

You have complete control over your data in FrontBack:

  • Delete Content: You can delete any photos or videos created with FrontBack directly from your Photos app
  • Revoke Permissions: You can revoke any granted permissions through iOS Settings at any time
  • Uninstall: Uninstalling the app removes all app data from your device
  • No Account Data: Since we don't require accounts, there is no personal data to export or delete from our servers
